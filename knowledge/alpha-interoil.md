## Our Growth and Evolution

Source: `/about#history`

- Our History
- Our Growth and Evolution
- More than two decades of operational experience, local capacity building, and sustainable growth in Nigeria.
- Over the years, Alpha Interoil expanded its operational footprint, developed a fleet of Nigerian-crewed vessels, and invested significantly in local capacity building, becoming a key contributor to Nigeria's maritime and offshore sectors.
- Following the global restructuring of the Bourbon Group in 2020, Alpha Interoil underwent strategic ownership and organizational changes. In 2022, the Company's shareholding structure was reconfigured to include Bourbon Mobility SAS, Evare Marine Services Limited, and local management interests.
- A significant milestone was reached in 2025 when Bourbon Mobility SAS initiated the divestment of its 40% stake in Alpha Interoil to Global Streams Marine Limited (GSML), marking the beginning of a new chapter in the Company's evolution while ensuring continuity of operations and long-term growth in Nigeria.
- Today, Alpha Interoil remains a trusted offshore logistics partner, leveraging over two decades of operational experience, a skilled Nigerian workforce, and a strong commitment to safety, operational excellence, and sustainable value creation.

## Training & Development

Source: `/services#training`

- Training & Development
- Bourbon Training Center
- Marine Training and Competence Development
- Building Competence, Enhancing Safety, and Driving Operational Excellence
- At Alpha Interoil , we are committed to developing highly competent, safety-conscious, and performance-driven marine professionals capable of meeting the demanding requirements of the offshore marine industry. Our Marine Training programme is designed to enhance crew competence, strengthen operational reliability, and promote a culture of continuous learning and operational excellence.
- Through a combination of practical deck and classroom training, simulator-based exercises, equipment familiarisation, and competency assessments, we equip our crew and personnel with the knowledge, technical skills, and confidence required to perform their duties safely and effectively in challenging offshore environments.
- Our Marine Training Capabilities
- Type C IMO Bridge Simulator Training: We deliver practical and live bridge simulator exercises for newly recruited and serving Captains, focusing on vessel manoeuvring, navigational decision-making, emergency response, bridge team management, and operational readiness.
- ECDIS Type-Specific Training: Our training includes familiarisation with the Furuno FMD-3200 Electronic Chart Display and Information System (ECDIS), supporting safe navigation, effective route planning, and the proper use of electronic navigation systems.
- Garmin Navigation Equipment Training: We provide practical familiarisation with Garmin navigation equipment to strengthen users' understanding of system functions and their effective application during vessel operations.
- GPS Training: Our GPS training develops personnel competence in position fixing, waypoint management, route monitoring, and the effective use of satellite-based positioning systems.
- Our Approach
- We believe that effective marine training goes beyond theoretical knowledge. Our approach combines structured instruction with practical exercises, scenario-based learning, equipment familiarisation, and competency evaluation. Training outcomes are reviewed to identify areas for improvement and ensure that learning translates into safer and more reliable onboard performance.
- By aligning our training activities with operational requirements, applicable maritime standards, client expectations, and industry best practices, Alpha Interoil continues to strengthen the competence of its marine workforce and support the delivery of dependable offshore marine services.
- Our Commitment
- At Alpha Interoil, competence is a cornerstone of safe operations. We remain committed to investing in our people, improving technical capabilities, and promoting professional excellence to ensure that our personnel are prepared to meet operational challenges while protecting lives, assets, and the marine environment.
